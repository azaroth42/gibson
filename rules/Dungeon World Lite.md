# Rules

## The Basics

You have six main statistics (often called stats, and typically abbreviated to three letters): 

* Strength (STR) 	\- How physically strong and powerful you are  
* Agility (AGL)		\- How nimble and graceful you are  
* Fortitude (FOR)	\- How healthy and resilient you are  
* Intelligence (INT)	\- How smart and clever you are  
* Perception (PER)	\- How observant and discerning you are  
* Charisma (CHA)	\- How charming and influencing you are

Statistics range from \-1 to \+3. When creating your character, you assign the following in any way you choose:  \+2, \+1, \+1, \+0, \+0, \-1

Much of the time you can describe what your character is doing, and they just do it. However if there is a potential conflict or uncertainty, then you can instead "make a move" using the following rules. All characters can use the Basic Moves, and every character has some additional moves that only they can use, given by their class.

Making a move: Roll 2d6 and add the stat given for the move. If the result is 6 or less, it's a miss, and the outcome is not going to be good. You might still succeed, but the costs will be high. On a 7, 8 or 9, it is a partial success: either there will be some difficult choice to make, some additional cost, or the effect will be reduced. On a 10 or higher, it is completely successful.

### Basic Moves

* **Attack Something:**   
  When you attack something, with or without a weapon, roll \+STR. On 7+ you deal damage. On 7-9, something bad also happens. On 6 or less, you miss the target, or you hit but doing so causes even more trouble.  
* **Avoid Trouble:**   
  When you try to avoid trouble, describe what you're doing. The GM will tell you which stat to roll, often \+AGL. On 10+ you avoid the trouble completely, on 7-9 choose from options the GM describes. On 6 or less, the trouble gets you.  
* **Survive:**   
  When you are reduced to 0 HP or below, roll \+FOR. On 7+, you're still up. On 7-9, you also take \-1 ongoing to all your rolls (including further Survive rolls). On 6 or less, you're down.  
* **Know Something:**   
  When you consult your own knowledge, a library or other source of information, roll \+INT. On 7+, ask a question and take \+1 forward. On 7-9, ask a question. On 6 or less, you might still know the answer to the question but it's going to be bad.  
* **Look Around:**  
  When you look around your current environment, roll \+PER. On 7+, ask a question. On 10+, ask another question after hearing the answer. On 6 or less, you likely notice trouble.  
* **Convince Someone:**   
  When you try to convince someone to do something, by fast-talking them, bribing them or intimidating them, roll \+CHA. On 7+ they do what you want. On 7-9, they need something extra. On 6 or less, they might attack you… or they might do what you want but twist your request to your peril.

### Damage and Hit Points

Every character has a certain tolerance for taking damage, called hit points (or "HP").

A character's maximum HP is: 15 \+ (5 x Fortitude) \+ level  
For example, a level 1 character with \+1 FOR has (15 \+ 5 \+ 1\) \= 21 HP.  A level 5 character with \+2 FOR has (15 \+ 10 \+ 5\) \= 30 HP. (Yes \-1 FOR means \-5 HP, so 11 at level 1\)

Dealing Damage:

When you deal damage (typically with Attack Something) you roll d6, add the stat for the move you used (typically STR), and add any bonuses from weapon(s) and/or abilities. The target then subtracts the armor value of any armor they're wearing and have naturally, then takes that much damage.

Taking Damage:

When you take damage, you subtract the amount taken from your current HP.  
When you take damage that reduces your HP to 0 or below, you have to roll Survive.  
If you take additional damage while at 0 HP or below, you're automatically down.  
If you take additional damage while down, you're dead. Sorry, but now you get to play a new character, which will also be fun\!

## Classes

Every character has a class, which gives them additional specific abilities or moves.

The classes are:

* Barbarian \- A warrior unfamiliar with civilization, and tends to fly into a deadly rage  
* Bard \- An artist and performer, who can inspire others and knows tales of old  
* Cleric \- A healer with powers given by their deity, especially against the undead  
* Druid \- A shapechanger, attuned to nature and the wilds  
* Fighter \- A swordsman with a powerful heirloom weapon  
* Knight \- A heavily armored defender of the weak  
* Martial Artist \- An expert in martial arts with supernatural control over their own body  
* Ninja \- A sneaky assassin who only needs to attack once from the shadows  
* Pirate \- Yaarhhh, a lightly armored swordsman with a sharp blade, and sharper tongue  
* Psionic \- Someone with mental powers, a psychic  
* Ranger \- A tracker and explorer of the wilderness  
* Thief \- A rogue and pickpocket	   
* Wizard \- An arcane magic user

## Races

Every character also is a particular race or species. Describe what sort of creature you are. 

Pick an ability relevant to your described race:

* Can see in pitch blackness (deep gnome, dark elf, ...)  
* Doesn't need to breathe (merfolk, undead, …)  
* Can fly short distances (kenku, angel, aarakokra, …)  
* \+1 armor vs one of: fire, cold, lightning, necrotic, bludgeoning, slashing (anything with tough skin, or from extreme locations)  
* Immune to poison and disease (dwarf, undead, …)  
* Has a close range breath weapon that deals d6+FOR damage of a particular type (dragonborn, mephit, …)  
* Ask a follow-up question for Know Something (Elf, …)  
* *Or suggest an ability appropriate to your race to the GM*

## Equipment

You start with two weapons, an armor, and a shield as desired.

### Weapons:  

Unarmed strikes: All d6s are converted to d3s (e.g. halve the number rolled)   
One handed weapons: Base damage  
Two handed weapons / Two weapons: Base damage \+2  
Bows: Attack at range with AGL for Base damage, uses two hands  
	Thrown weapons: Close range base damage weapons  
	Wand: One handed implement for wizards: Base damage  
	Staff: Two handed implement for wizards: Base damage \+1

### Armor:  

Clothing: None  
Light Armor: \+1 armor  
Heavy Armor: \+2 armor, but \-1 to AGL rolls  
	Shield: \+1 armor (only for 1 handed weapons)

## Advancement

At the end of each adventure you gain a level and can pick an advancement:

Level 2 and above:  
\[\] Advance a stat from \-1 or 0  
\[\] Advance a stat from \-1,0, or \+1  
\[\] \[\] \[\] Advanced: Gain an advanced move  
\[\] Multiclass: Gain a base move from another class

Level 6 and above  
\[\] Advance a stat from \-1,0, or \+1  
\[\] Advance a stat from \-1,0,+1 or \+2  
\[\] \[\] \[\] Expert: Gain an expert or advanced move  
\[\] Advanced Multiclass: Gain an advanced move from your second class

Level 11 and above  
\[\] Advance a stat from \-1,0,+1 or \+2  
\[\] \[\] \[\] Master: Gain an expert or advanced move  
\[\] Expert Multiclass: Gain an expert move from your second class

Common Advanced Moves  
\[\] Deal \+2 damage   
\[\] Reroll 1s on damage  
\[\] Lucky: Reroll a roll once per session  
\[\] \+5 HP

Common Expert Moves  
\[\] Deal \+d6 damage (instead of \+2)  
\[\] \+1 piercing  
\[\] Reroll 2s on damage  
\[\] \+5 HP  
\[\] \+1 armor


## The Classes and their Moves

### Barbarian

**Rage:** At the beginning of the session, roll \+FOR. On a 7-9 hold 1, on 10+ hold 3, on a miss hold 1\. During the session you can spend a hold, while not wearing heavy armor, to add \+2 armor against an attack or to deal maximum damage.

**Break Things:** When you use pure strength to destroy something, roll \+STR. On a 7-9 choose 2, on a 10+ choose 3:  
	\* It doesn't take very long.   
	\* Nothing else of value is damaged  
	\* It doesn't make a lot of noise  
	\* It can be fixed without a lot of effort

Advanced \- **Berzerk Rage:** When you Break Things or take 4 or more damage, gain one rage. 

Advanced \- **Smash\!:** When you roll 12+ on Attack Something, or 10+ and spend a rage, you can also destroy an object held by, worn by or near your target.

Advanced \- **Antimagical:** If you roll a 6 or less to Avoid Trouble for magic, instead it is treated as a 7-9. You always receive 2 fewer HP (minimum 0\) from magical healing.

Expert \- **Too Angry to Die:** You can spend a hold to reroll Survive. While you have 6 or fewer HP and hold at least one rage, you have \+1 to Attack Something.

Expert \- **Taste for Blood:** When you kill a foe and taste their blood, gain 2d6 HP and 2 rage.

Expert \- **More the Merrier:** When foes outnumber you, add your current rage to your damage.

Master \- **Shatter the World:** You can Break Things up to castle size, or are magical. Pick an additional option on a hit. Add the following possible options: 

* It is moved rather than destroyed  
* It's like it never existed


### Bard

**Inspire:** When you perform your art to inspire others, roll \+CHA. On a 7-9, a number of targets up to your CHA get \+1 forward on their next roll, until your next turn. On a 10+, they also head 1d6 HP

**Famous:** When you meet someone, declare whether or not they know you. The GM will tell you what they know about you.

Advanced \- **Perform:** When you perform your art for an audience, roll \+CHA. On a 7-9 pick 1, on 10+ pick 2\. You gain: 

* 1 Treasure  
* The admiration of a powerful NPC  
* useful information  
* temporary lackeys  
* fame in the current city or area 

Advanced \- **Here's the Plan:** When you make a plan and assign tasks to others, roll \+CHA. On a 10+, everyone holds 3\. On a 7-9, everyone holds 1\. Spend a hold, while following the plan, to roll with Advantage. If the plan succeeds, the GM will be generous with rewards.

Advanced \- **Serenity:** While you play a calming melody, all Attacks have Disadvantage, and all damage from NPCs and monsters is at \-3

Expert \- **Expert Motivator:** When you Inspire others, they deal \+d6 damage and they are healed (possibly an additional) d6 HP.

Expert \- **Memorable:** You roll with Advantage to Convince people who have heard you Perform.

Expert \- **Virtuoso:** Pick an additional option for Perform and add these options. The audience is:

* aggressive towards something of your choice  
* protective of you and your friends  
* entranced by you and your performance


Master \- **Winner's Tale:** When you tell of history, roll \+CHA. Your story is the new, unchangeable truth, the GM will explain differences in the world. On a 7-9, some of the changes are unwanted. 


### Cleric

**Heal:** When you cast this, touch the target and roll \+CHA. ON a 7-9, heal d6+CHA. On a 10+, heal 2d6+CHA. If the party rests for 10 minutes, everyone heals fully.

**Turn Undead:** When you cast this, brandish the symbol of your deity towards 1 or more undead creatures and roll \+CHA. On a 7-9, the targets cannot move closer to you and low-level undead might flee. On a 10+, you choose whether targets below your level flee, are paralyzed, or take d6+CHA damage, ignoring armor. The GM chooses for targets above your level.

Advanced: **Advanced Healing:** When you Heal, on a 7-9 instead heal 2d6 \+CHA. On a 10+, heal 3d6+CHA and heal any poison or disease

Advanced: **Hold Person:** When you cast this to stop target humanoid from moving, roll \+CHA. On a 7-9, the target is slowed (attacks against get \+1). On a 10+, the target is paralyzed (attacks against roll with advantage). It lasts for CHA rounds.

Advanced: **Magic Weapons**: When you bless a number of non-magical weapons, roll \+CHA. On a 7-9, you can bless CHA weapons. On a 10+, you can bless CHA+2 weapons. Blessed weapons deal \+2 damage, count as magical, and give \+1 to Attack Something against undead creatures.

Expert: **Healing Circle:** When you Heal, it affects CHA+1 targets.

Expert: **Flamestrike**: When you Attack Something by casting this, roll \+CHA. You deal 3d6+CHA holy fire damage to a single target at up to medium range, ignoring armor. On a 10+, if the target is undead, it is destroyed

Expert \- **Holy Aura:** You have a 15' aura, in which allies heal 1 HP on each of your turns, undead cannot enter it and would take d6 damage while within it. Allies within it attack with Advantage. 

Master \- **Miracle:** When you cast this, roll \+CHA. The target: is raised from the dead; is fully healed; can reroll a move. On a 7-9 also pick: A magical item is drained; \-1 to a stat until you rest for a week; \-2 to cast any spell for a day.


### Druid 

**Shapechange:** When you want to change your shape to that of an animal you know, roll \+FOR. On 7+, you change, your unarmed strikes deal d6 damage, and you gain a special power assigned by the GM. On a 7-9 hold 2, on a 10+ hold 3\. Use this hold to activate the power. After you use your last hold, you return to your normal form.

**Wild Knowledge:** When you Know Something or Look Around for plants or animals, and get 7+, you can ask an additional question.

Advanced \- **Advanced Shapechanger:** Hold 2+FOR on a 7-9, or 3+FOR on a 10+, for Shapechange.

Advanced \- **Magical Claws:** While shapechanged, you retain the bonuses and abilities of any weapons you were wielding at the time you changed. Your claws are always magical

Advanced \- **Plant Lord:** You can speak with plants, and can shapechange into a natural plant form.

Expert \- **Wildshape:** Until you use all your hold, you can change shape without rolling.  
	  
Expert \- **Pack Mind:** When you mark an animal, roll \+FOR. On a 7-9, pick one. One a 10+ pick two. This lasts until you mark another animal.

* You can see through the animals eyes.  
* You can communicate telepathically with it.  
* You can recall its memories.

Expert \- **Barkskin:** You gain \+1 armor. When shapechanged, you gain \+2 armor instead.

Master \- **Elemental Chimera:** When you Shapechange, you can create a form with features of up to three animals or raw elements such as fire, water, or lightning. Each feature grants a different ability. You gain an additional two hold.

### Fighter   

**Cleave:** When you Attack Something with a melee weapon and get 10+, deal the same damage again to a different, adjacent target.

**Ancestral Weapon:** Pick a base weapon type. Give the weapon a name and a look. It counts as magical. Add one the these options: 

* \+2 damage and heavy  
* \+2 damage and messy  
* \+1 piercing  
* Uses AGL instead of STR to Attack  
* Advantage to Attack against (and optionally glows in the presence of) a creature type.

Advanced \- **Improved Weapon**: Pick another option for your weapon.

Advanced \- **Commune:** When you commune with your ancestors who wielded your weapon before you, roll \+CHA. On a 7-9, you can ask them a question. On a 10+, you can ask a second question. You take Advantage if you act directly on the answers. 

Expert \- **Two Weapons:** Pick another option for your weapon, or gain a second weapon with two options.

Expert \- **Blacksmith:** When you destroy a magic weapon to add its powers to your weapon, roll \+FOR. On a 10+, everything is added to your ancestral weapon. On a 7-9, something is left out, weakened, or carries a new penalty for use.

Expert \- **Razor Sharp:** Your weapon deals \+d6 damage, \+1 piercing

Master \- **Vorpal Blade**: When you Attack with your weapon, on a 12+ you deal double damage, ignoring all armor


### Knight

**Armor Knowledge:** You ignore the AGL penalty of Heavy Armor.

**Charge\!:** When you lead a group into battle, you and those you lead take \+1 on their first Attack Something roll, and deal \+2 damage if they hit

Advanced \- **Smite:** When you deal damage to a foe that opposes your patron's ideals (GM's call) deal an additional 2d6 of damage, once per combat. 

Advanced \- **Lord's Favor:** Whenever you act to protect the weak or avenge them, you have \+1 armor and deal \+1 damage.  

Advanced \- **Defend:** When you stand in defence of someone or thing, roll \+FOR. On a 7-9, hold 1\. On a 10+ hold 3\. Until your next turn, you can spend hold to redirect an attack from who/what you are defending to yourself, or to gain \+1 armor against an attack.

Expert \- **Guardian:** When you Defend, hold 1+FOR on a 7-9, and 3+FOR on a 10+

Expert \- **Bastion:** You are always immune to poison, disease, fear, charm, slow, paralysis, and being stunned.

Expert \- **Cavalry:** When you Attack from horseback, you deal \+d6 damage and roll with Advantage.

Master \- **Invulnerable:** When you heroically Defend someone or something against terrible odds, you become immune to all damage and effects. You must spend a hold each turn to maintain this. On a 7-9, when the Invulnerability ends you are Weakened (-1 STR) until you sleep.


### Monk

**Flying Fists:** When you Attack Something with your fists and feet, and don't use armor or a shield, you can roll \+AGL and deal damage as if they were two small weapons (d6+2).

**Chi Strike:** When you Attack Something with an unarmed strike, on a 7-9 you can choose one of the following instead of dealing damage. On a 10+, you deal damage as well.

* The target is temporarily stunned  
* The target drops whatever they are holding  
* The target is knocked backwards or falls over

Advanced \- **Quivering Palm:** Add the following Chi Strike options: 

* The target cannot speak.  
* The target cannot move. 

Advanced \- **Flow like Water:** While unarmored, you gain 1 Armor. If you roll a miss in combat for any roll, hold 1\. You can spend the hold during the combat to add d6 damage when you hit.

Advanced \- **Mountain's Stillness:** You can Avoid Trouble with FOR to heal d6+FOR HP, including in place of a Survive roll. If afterwards you are still at 0 or below, you need to make a Survive check as normal.

Expert \- **Fists of Fire:** You summon fire around your fists that deals an extra d6 damage and your attacks count as magical. Add the Chi Strike option: The target is set on fire.

Expert \- **Catching Flies:** When you catch a missile fired at you, roll \+AGL. On a 7-9, you knock it aside and avoid the damage. On a 10+, you catch and throw it back at your attacker doing regular damage. 

Expert \- **Iron Body:** You gain \+1 Armor and can never be knocked over or pushed backwards.

Master \- **Flurry of Blows:** Your unarmed strikes can target all opponents within reach, and you can select two Chi Strike options for each.


### Ninja

**Ninja Reflexes:** When you use Avoid Trouble for acrobatics, climbing or stealth, when not using heavy armor, you have \+1 to the roll. 

**Sneak Attack:** When you hit with Attack Something and the target is unaware of you, add \+d6 damage.	

Advanced \- **Outta Here:** When you flee the scene, roll \+AGL. On a 10+, you're gone. On a 7-9, you can leave, but if you go, you leave something behind, or take something with you, the GM will tell you what.

Advanced \- **Shot from the Dark:** When you Attack Something and the target is unaware of you, take \+1 on the roll.

Advanced \- **Shadow Blade:** When you cover your weapon in shadows, roll \+INT. Take \+1 on the roll if you are in dim light or a shadowy area. On a 7-9, it gains \+2 damage and \+2 piercing. On a 10+, \+d6 damage and ignores armor. It lasts until the end of the scene, or in bright light only for one attack.

Expert \- **Shadow Form:** You can remain hidden after an attack.

Expert \- **Shadow Step:** When you teleport between nearby shadows, roll \+AGL. On a 10+ you make the step successfully. On a 7-9, either something comes with you from the shadows, or your draw attention to yourself.  
	  
Expert \- **Shadow Clone:** When you make a duplicate of yourself out of shadow, roll \+INT. It lasts until hit. On a 10+, you can swap places with the clone at will.

Master \- **Defy Gravity:** You can Avoid Trouble to walk along walls or the ceiling, while all you are doing is moving.


### Pirate

**Finesse:** When you Attack Something with a balanced melee weapon, while not using a shield or heavy armor, you can roll \+AGL instead.

**Parry:** Once per turn, when you are the target of a melee attack, you can Avoid Trouble to parry the attack using a balanced weapon.

Advanced \- **Witty Repartee:** When you take a moment in battle to subtly mock a foe, roll \+CHA. On a 7-9 pick 1, on a 10+ pick 2:

* All take \+1 forward against the target  
* The target makes a mistake  
* The target doesn't notice you doing it  
* You impress others


Advanced \- **Cutting Rebuke:** Add \+CHA to your damage with balanced weapons

Advanced \- **Ta-dah\!**: When you make a showy entrance into a dangerous or important situation, roll \+CHA.  On a 7-9, you pick one target to stop, watch and listen. On a 10+, everyone does.

Expert \- **En Garde:** When you challenge a foe to single combat, roll \+CHA. On a 7+, they are compelled to fight you. On a 10+, you roll Attack Something with Advantage while you, and only you, are attacking them.

Expert \- **Chandelier Swing:** When you use the surroundings to your advantage when moving, roll \+AGL. On a 7-9 pick 1, on a 10+ pick 2:

* You arrive anywhere nearby, unscathed  
* You deal base damage to an opponent  
* You put an opponent at a disadvantage  
* You gain the support or admiration of onlookers

Expert \- **I Meant To Do That:** When you roll 6- on a physical action, describe how your failure leads to a different, unintended advantage. The GM will offer you a complicated success.

Master \- **Calculated Chaos:** When you embark on a needlessly complicated plan relying on luck, describe the end result and roll \+CHA. On a 12+, it works exactly as described. On a 10-12, there's a cost. On a 7-9, the cost is high.	

### Psionic

**Mind Control:** When you silently Convince Someone by telepathyl, you can roll \+INT instead of \+CHA.

**Mind Blast:** When you Attack Something by casting this, you can roll \+INT instead of \+STR. The attack is up to medium range and deals base damage, ignoring armor.

Advanced \- **Telekinesis:** When you use your mind to move an object, roll \+INT. On a 7-9 pick 2, on a 10+ pick 3: 

* The object can be heavy  
* You can move it quickly  
* You can move it precisely  
* You can move it quietly  
* There's no collateral damage  
   

Advanced \- **Psychometry:** When you examine an object to understand its history or recent use, roll \+INT. On a 7-9 ask one question. One a 10+ ask a follow up or second question. 

Advanced \- **Precognition:** At the beginning of the session, roll \+INT. On a 10+ hold 3\. On 7-9, hold 1\. Spend a hold to gain \+2 armor, \+1 forward, or have exactly the needed non-magical item.

Expert \- **Astral Projection:** When you send your spirit away from your body, roll \+INT. Your spirit is insubstantial and on a 7-9 pick two, on a 10+ pick three. Your spirit is also: 

* invisible  
* fast  
* clear-sighted  
* able to be gone for a long time.  
   

Expert \- **Telepathic Link:** When you link people together telepathically, roll \+INT. On a 7-9, you can link 1+INT people, on a 10+ 3+INT. All linked can communicate telepathically, use the highest INT for Know Something rolls, and ask an additional question for Look Around. Other situational effects are possible at the GM's discretion.

Expert \- **Hallucinations:** When you project an illusion into a target's mind, roll \+INT. Describe the illusion the target sees. On a 7-9, it's either very simple or only distracting. On a 10+ it's entirely believable.  
	  
Master \- **Psychic Thief:** When you touch a target and steal an intangible (voice, memory, ability, rank, sight, courage, etc), roll \+INT. On 10+ you have it, they don't. On a 7-9, you have \-1 INT while you have it. It returns at sunrise.

### Ranger

**Keen Eyes:** When you Attack Something with a bow, you may roll \+PER.

**Tracking:** When you try to track your quarry or find a safe path for your party through dangerous terrain, roll \+PER. On 7-9 pick one:

* It takes longer than expected  
* You leave something or someone behind  
* Something or someone follows you.


Advanced \- **Called Shot:** When you Attack with a bow, pick an option. On a 7-9, you deal damage or cause the effect. On a 10+, you do both. The target:

* is stunned  
* drops what they're holding  
* is knocked back or falls over.

Advanced \- **Arrow Storm**: When you Attack with a bow, deal the same damage to another target.

Advanced \- **Animal Empathy**: You can speak with and understand normal animals.

Expert \- **Exploit Weakness**: When you attack with a bow, you ignore armor.

Expert \- **Read the Terrain**: When you enter a new area, ask 2 questions from:

* What is dangerous here?  
* Where is safe here?  
* What is not what it appears to be here?

	  
Expert \- **Animal Companion**: You have an animal companion. Describe it. If it attacks with you, deal \+d6 damage. You get \+1 to Tracking, and ask an extra question for Look Around. You can communicate with it telepathically.

Master \- **One with Nature:** When you slay a powerful monster and become one with it, roll \+FOR. On a 10+, you gain an innate power. On a 7-9, it will require an additional sacrifice.


### Thief

**Sleight of Hand:** When you unlock a lock, pickpocket someone, disarm a trap or disable a device, roll \+AGL. On a 7-9 pick one: 

* It takes longer than expected  
* it's noisier than expected  
* you or something else is damaged in the process


**Sneaky:** You have \+1 to Avoid Trouble for stealth, \+1 to Look Around and Know Something for valuable items, security and escape routes.

Advanced \- **Connections:** When you seek to acquire knowledge or an item from other criminals, roll \+CHA. On a 10+, someone has it for you. On a 7-9, you'll have to settle for something close, or there'll be additional costs.

Advanced \- **Awareness:** You're never surprised, and always go first in battle.

Advanced \- **Poisoner:** When you apply poisons to your blade, roll \+AGL. On a 7-9, it either takes two doses or you get some on yourself suffering a mild version of the effect. When you take this advance, pick two poisons you can brew.

Expert \- **Disguise:** When you have the time and materials to disguise yourself, roll \+AGL. On a 7-9, pick one: 

* It doesn't last very long  
* It's not convincing up close  
* It's going to cost you extra to finish it.

Expert \- **Nose for Treasure:** Whenever you find or receive Treasure, you find one more.

Expert \- **Mother Duck:** When you are stealthy and want to bring others with you, roll \+PER. On a 7-9, you can bring one person along. On a 10+ you can bring two people.

Master \- **One Step Ahead:** When you roll 6 or less for Avoid Trouble and explain how you were prepared for it in advance, instead treat the roll as if it was a 7\.


### Wizard

**Attuned Senses:** When you Look Around, you can also detect magic.

**Magic Missile:** When you Attack Something by casting this, roll \+INT instead of \+STR. The attack can be at any range and has an additional \+1 piercing. The type of damage is determined by your implement (a wand or staff).

Advanced \- **Mage Armor:** When you summon magical armor by casting this, roll \+INT. On a 7-9, gain 1 armor. On a 10+ gain 2 armor. This armor lasts until the end of the scene and cannot be stacked.

Advanced \- **Invisibility:** When you cast Invisibility on a target, including yourself, roll \+INT. On a 7+, the target is invisible until it attacks or until the end of the scene. On a 10+, the target also takes \+1 forward. On a 6-, you and the target *think* they're invisible.

Advanced \- **Energy Burst:** When you Attack Something by casting this, roll \+INT. The attack affects everything within a 15' radius sphere up to medium range, deals base \+d6 damage, and has \+1 piercing. The type of damage is determined by your implement.

Expert \- **Dimension Door:** When you create a doorway between two places up to medium range, that you can step through, roll \+INT. On a 7+ the doorway is created, and you can step through it. On a 10+ you can allow others to step through it as well until you next take a turn.

Expert \- **Chain Strike:** When you Attack Something by casting this, roll \+INT. On a 7-9 the attack affects up to 1+INT targets, on a 10+ it affects up to 3+INT targets. It deals base damage \+d6, and has \+2 piercing. The type of damage is determined by your implement.

Expert \- **Globe of Invulnerability:** When you cast this, roll \+INT. On a 7+, you are immune to damage caused by magic, and gain \+1 armor (stacking with Mage Armor). On a 10+, gain \+2 armor instead. The globe lasts until the end of the scene.

Master \- **Ritual:** When you spend an hour or more to create a long lasting or powerful effect, roll \+INT. On a 10+ the GM will tell you 1-2 requirements, on a 7-9, 2-4. When the requirements are met, the effect happens.
